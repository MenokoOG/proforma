Method

Three reasons the numbers hold up

Most AI business cases fall over in the same place: benefits estimated more generously than costs, annual figures starting in the wrong year, and nobody able to name the peak funding need.

Eight steps

Follows a real approval conversation

Brief, use case, architecture, costs, benefits, risks, results, roadmap. Then export to PDF, Markdown, CSV or a JSON project file that round-trips everything.

Verified arithmetic

Checked against the source workbook

Every figure derives from something you entered. A regression suite guards payback, ROI, NPV and IRR so a refactor cannot quietly move a number.

Local first

Your figures never leave the device

Runs entirely in your browser with React as the only runtime dependency. No backend, no sign-up, and it keeps working offline.
