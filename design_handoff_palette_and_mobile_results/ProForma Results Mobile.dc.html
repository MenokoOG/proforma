What changed

Chart redrawn for the viewport

The 720×320 viewBox scaled every label down to about 4.8px. This one is 360×260 — roughly 1:1 at 375px — so the 11px labels stay 11px. Gridlines drop from five to two plus the zero line, and each year's running total sits under its bar, signed and colored.

Figures no longer break mid-number

The hero keeps its full figure on one line at full width. The six secondary stats drop to compact notation ($4.49M) with white-space: nowrap instead of overflow-wrap: anywhere.

Table becomes a year list

Five cards instead of a 560px-wide grid you scroll sideways. Each shows the year's net and running total; tap to expand the lines behind it. Break-even year is marked in place. The full table is still one tap away for anyone who wants it.

Palette is still the current one — that's the separate question. This is the layout only.
